Moamalat Payment Provider

Take card payments in Libya through the Moamalat Lightbox — without leaving the checkout

The customer picks Moamalat, the hosted card form opens over the checkout page, and they pay. Card details go straight to Moamalat and never touch your Odoo server. It is an ordinary Odoo payment provider: it works anywhere payment works — eCommerce checkout, customer portal invoices, subscriptions and payment links.

How a payment goes through

1 Customer picks Moamalat at checkout.
2 Odoo signs the request with your Secure Key and opens the Lightbox.
3 The customer enters their card in Moamalat's own form. The transaction becomes Pending.
4 Moamalat posts a signed notification to your webhook. Odoo verifies it and sets the transaction Done, and the order confirms.

Only a signed notification can settle an order

The Lightbox reports its result through the customer's browser. That report is treated as a hint and nothing more: it moves the customer along to the status page, but it cannot mark a payment received. The state of a transaction changes only when Moamalat posts a notification whose HMAC-SHA256 signature verifies against your Notification Key.

This matters because the browser is not a trusted messenger. If a claim of “paid” coming from it were believed, anyone who could guess an order reference could confirm an order without paying for it.

What you need

From Moamalat

A Merchant ID and Terminal ID, a Secure Key for signing payment requests, and a Notification Key for verifying the webhook. Both keys are hexadecimal; Odoo refuses to save one that is not.

In your Odoo

Register /payment/moamalat/webhook with Moamalat as the notification URL. Without it, payments stay Pending rather than confirming — which is the safe direction to fail, but it does mean the webhook is not optional.

Details worth knowing

Currencies. Offered only for LYD, USD and EUR, so it never appears at checkout in a currency Moamalat would reject.

Refunds. Partial and full refunds are sent to Moamalat from the transaction, as with any Odoo provider.

Test mode. Set the provider to Test to use Moamalat's staging host. The Lightbox is loaded from the host that matches the provider's state, so there is nothing else to switch over when you go live.

No card data. Tokenization and express checkout are off: the card is entered in Moamalat's form, so your Odoo never sees or stores it.
