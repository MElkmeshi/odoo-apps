Plutu Payment Provider

Take payments in Libya through Plutu — local bank cards, T-Lync, international cards, Adfali and Sadad

For cards and T-Lync the customer is sent to Plutu to pay, and Plutu reports the result back with a signed callback. Adfali and Sadad never leave the checkout: Plutu texts the customer a code, they type it in, and the payment settles. It is an ordinary Odoo payment provider: it works anywhere payment works — eCommerce checkout, customer portal invoices, subscriptions and payment links.

Five gateways

Local Bank Cards Libyan bank cards on the Numo network.
T-Lync The T-Lync wallet. Needs the customer's mobile number.
Mastercard / Visa International cards through MPGS.
Adfali Pays by one-time code, without leaving the checkout.
Sadad Pays by one-time code. Asks for a birth year as well as a number.

Each is a separate payment method, so you can switch any of them on or off independently from the provider's configuration.

Every callback is verified

A payment is only ever settled from a callback whose HMAC-SHA256 signature verifies against your Plutu secret key. An unsigned or altered callback is refused and the transaction is left alone.

The signature is rebuilt per gateway and per channel, exactly as Plutu's own PHP SDK builds it. This matters more than it sounds: T-Lync signs a different set of fields on the browser return than on the server callback, and unlike the other gateways it does not sign canceled at all. A single shared field list verifies one gateway and silently rejects the next.

What you need

From Plutu

An API Key, an Access Token and a Secret Key, from your Plutu merchant account. Plutu also whitelists the IP your Odoo calls from, so add your server's address in their dashboard.

In your Odoo

Set the provider to Enabled and fill in the three keys. The provider installs disabled and without credentials, so nothing can take money until you have deliberately turned it on.

Details worth knowing

Currency. Plutu settles in Libyan Dinar, so the provider is offered only for LYD and never appears at checkout in a currency it would reject.

T-Lync needs a phone number. If the customer has none on file, the payment stops in Odoo with a message they can act on, rather than failing on Plutu's page.

No card data. The customer pays on Plutu's page, so your Odoo never sees or stores a card. Tokenization and express checkout are off for the same reason.

Adfali and Sadad ask for a mobile number. Plutu texts a code to it — four digits for Adfali, six for Sadad — which the customer types into the checkout. Sadad also asks for a birth year, and only accepts numbers starting 091 or 093. Nothing is charged until the code is accepted.
